# Sample tour photos

Drop four landscape images here and they get attached to the demo tours as hero
images, which is what the "Your listings" cards on Home show.

    migration.jpg      Great Migration — Northern Serengeti
    ngorongoro.jpg     Ngorongoro & Tarangire Family Safari
    photography.jpg    Photographers' Serengeti
    kilimanjaro.jpg    Kilimanjaro — Machame Route

Landscape, at least 1200px wide, roughly 3:2. Any name works — the mapping above
is just the order they are wired in.

**They must be images you own or have a licence to use in marketing.** These end
up in the App Store screenshots, which are public. Two sources that look
available but are not:

- `makutano-marketplace/static/theme/img/` — the purchased theme ships no real
  photography at all. 155 files share 35 checksums and every one is a flat grey
  dimension placeholder; `eiffel.jpg` and `notredame.jpg` are the same file.
- The tour photos on the live marketplace belong to the operators who uploaded
  them. They are a real customer's work and cannot appear under a fictional
  operator's name.

Without images the cards fall back to a placeholder icon over the accent wash.
The title, country, duration and price still render, so the screenshot is honest
either way — just weaker.
