Makutano Connect brings your customer conversations, orders and bookings together in one place, so nothing is forgotten between a message and a payment.

Open the app and the first thing you see is what needs you today: a payment waiting to be checked, a quotation the customer has not answered, an order ready to go out. Under it, the customers you were already in the middle of — with what stage each one has reached, not just who messaged last.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Read and reply to customer messages from your WhatsApp Business number, in one shared inbox
• See every enquiry, quotation, booking and order with its next step already worked out
• Take a conversation, or hand it to a colleague, and know who is answering
• Log an enquiry that arrived by phone or in person, in a few taps
• Get notified the moment a customer writes to a chat you are responsible for
• Follow payments from request to confirmation, with money always verified by a person

BUILT FOR HOW A SMALL BUSINESS ACTUALLY WORKS

Your team sees what their job needs. An owner sees the whole business; a consultant sees the customers assigned to them; whoever checks the money sees payments waiting to be verified. Nobody is shown a screen they cannot act on, and private conversations stay private.

The app adapts to what your business does. A tour operator sees enquiries, quotations and bookings. A shop sees orders and deliveries. You are never asked to work around features meant for someone else's business.

WORKS WITH YOUR EXISTING SETUP

Makutano Connect is the phone companion to the Makutano Connect web portal. Enquiries that arrive through your website, your WhatsApp number or your own systems all land in the same inbox, and anything you do on the phone is on the web the moment you look.

BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

This app is for businesses already using Makutano Connect. You will need an account created by your business — you cannot sign up inside the app. To set up a workspace for your business, visit connect.makutano.co.tz.
